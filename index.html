Programme of Events

Licensed Bar & Rules

Escapade operates a fully licensed lounge bar offering craft beer, wine, cider, and non-alcoholic options.

Strict No-BYO Policy: Outside alcohol is prohibited by NZ licensing law.

Sobriety Policy: Intoxicated players cannot participate in the escape rooms.

Comfort & Lockers

Preparation tips for enjoying your escape room experience:

  • Secure Lockers: Complimentary lockers are provided inside the lounge for bags and jackets.
  • Footwear: Flat, comfortable footwear is recommended as you will be exploring clues.
  • Team Sizes: Up to 6 themed rooms accommodate groups from 2 to 6 participants per room.
No intense physical exertion required — purely observation & puzzle solving!
